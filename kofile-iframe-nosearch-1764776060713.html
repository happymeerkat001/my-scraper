Announcements
Welcome to the Limestone County Clerk’s Public Records Online Search!
With a credit card or charge account, customers can obtain copies of the instruments recorded in our office for $1/page.
Marriage License applications can be completed on our site and submitted to our office.
Marriage License application requirements and instructions are also listed on our website.

On the external website, images of the instruments will only be available as thumbnail sized.
Full-size images can be viewed on the public computers located in our office.
Certified copies of Land Documents and Vital Records can be requested by calling or visiting our office.

We are actively working to add the index information to all of our documents and some documents older than 1954 may show to be indexed.
Index and Images are available as described below:
Land Records
Index Data: 1954 through Verified Date
Images: from Sovereign through Verified Date

Assumed Names
Index Data: 01/01/2005 through Verified Date
Images: 01/01/2005 through Verified Date

Cattle Brands
Index Data: 12/9/2019 through Verified Date
Images: 12/9/2019 through Verified Date

Marriage Records
Index Data: 1/1/2005 through Current Date
Images: Not available to view on the website.

Military Discharges – Contact our office to obtain a copy.

Please contact our office should you have any questions regarding recording fees or obtaining certified copies.
For Technical Support, please send an email to gssupport@kofile.us or click on the ‘Need Help?’ button on the task bar.
